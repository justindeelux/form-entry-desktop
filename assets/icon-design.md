# Form Entry Desktop app icon

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Green spreadsheet → white arrow → purple form with a completion check, on a navy tile. Transparent outer margin; no text or Google logo.

`app-icon.png` is the 1024px master used by the app window and macOS Dock. `app-icon.icns` contains standard macOS sizes; `app-icon.ico` contains 16, 32, 128 and 256px Windows sizes. Forge uses the same filename base for both platforms.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: production desktop app icon, square 1024x1024 PNG with transparent outer background.
Primary request: Create a polished distinctive app icon for Form Entry Desktop, a minimal desktop utility that maps spreadsheet columns to form fields and automatically submits rows. A unified bold symbol: a green spreadsheet panel on the left flows through a short clear rightward arrow into a purple form panel on the right. Panels gently overlap into one cohesive silhouette; spreadsheet has a simple 2-by-3 grid, form has three bold short input lines and one small completed check mark. No text.
Style: premium macOS productivity icon, restrained softly dimensional geometry, crisp edges, gentle bevels and subtle depth; no busy illustration.
Composition: centered on a deep midnight navy rounded-square tile, tile occupies about 88% of canvas with transparent margin around all sides, generous internal padding, front-facing. Bright green and violet panels plus white arrow. Large simple shapes legible at 32px. The icon itself is the entire image, no mockup or presentation frame.
Constraints: actual transparent background outside the tile, no letters, no words, no numbers, no Google logo, no watermark, no extra objects.
